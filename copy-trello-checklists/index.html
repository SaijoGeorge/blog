Copy Trello Checklists

This bookmarklet simplifies the process of copying checklists from one Trello card to another. No need to fumble around with the "Copy items from…" dropdown with a gazillion checklists to choose from. The tool is also capable of handling multiple checklists, enabling you to copy all the checklist items to your clipboard and then effortlessly paste them into a different list.

Copy Trello Checklists v3.0

Tip: If the button won't drag, right-click it and choose "Bookmark This Link".

How it works

  1. Drag the bookmarklet button to your bookmarks bar
  2. Open any Trello card with checklists
  3. Click the bookmarklet in your bookmarks
  4. Select your copy mode (All/Unchecked/Checked items)
  5. Optionally enable numbering
  6. Click "Copy to Clipboard"
  7. Paste the checklist items wherever you need them

Limitations: Strikethrough formatting is not preserved when pasting. Items will copy as plain text with markdown formatting for bold, italics, and links.

Easiest way to copy paste Trello checklists

Trello's native "Copy items from…" feature becomes unwieldy when you have multiple checklists across different cards. This bookmarklet eliminates the hassle by copying all checklist items directly to your clipboard in one click.

Powerful features

This isn't just a simple copy tool. It includes smart features to give you complete control over how your checklists are copied.

Three Copy Modes
Choose to copy all items, only ☐ unchecked items, or only ☑ checked items
Format Preservation
Keeps bold, italics, code formatting, and clickable links intact
Auto Numbering
Optionally add sequential numbers to your checklist items
Smart Scrolling
Automatically loads lazy-loaded items before copying
