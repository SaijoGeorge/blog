AI Words Detector

Easiest way to spot AI words and phrases in webpages and Google Docs.

You know that feeling when you're reading something and it just... sounds like AI? Words like delve, multifaceted, and those telltale em-dashes — everywhere. This free Chrome extension highlights them instantly on any webpage, so you can spot AI patterns before your readers do.

Add to Chrome (Free)

Works on Google Docs, WordPress, competitor sites, and pretty much anywhere.

How to use AI Words Detector

  1. Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store
  2. Visit any webpage you want to analyse
  3. Click the extension icon to open the side panel
  4. Watch as AI-typical words get highlighted instantly
  5. Click any word in the panel to jump through occurrences
  6. Add your own custom words to track (like client no-go terms)

Spot AI writing patterns instantly

AI-generated content has tells. Certain words appear far more frequently in LLM output than in human writing. Once you see them, you can't unsee them. This extension makes those patterns visible at a glance.

  • 250+ pre-loaded AI buzzwords like "delve", "tapestry", "nuanced", "multifaceted"
  • Catches em-dashes (—) which LLMs use excessively
  • Add your own words to track, like client-specific no-go terms
  • Works on Google Docs, WordPress, and tricky SPAs

Features that actually help

This isn't some bloated tool that tries to do everything. It does one thing well: highlight words you need to pay attention to.

Jump Navigation

Click any word in the sidebar to jump through each occurrence on the page. No more Ctrl+F hunting.

Two Colour Sets

Default AI words get yellow highlights. Your custom words get purple. Easy to tell apart.

Word Counts

See exactly how many times each flagged word appears. Sort by count or alphabetically.

Toggle On/Off

Turn individual words or all highlights on and off. Helpful when the page gets busy.

Catch hidden terms you can't see

AI-generated content sometimes hides in collapsed sections or elements with zero opacity. This extension detects those hidden terms and makes them visible, so you can find every instance of AI patterns; even the ones deliberately concealed.

  • Detects terms in collapsed or hidden HTML elements (display:none, zero opacity)
  • Click detected terms to highlight and scroll to the section containing them
  • Expand collapsed sections to reveal the actual hidden text in context
  • Never miss AI patterns buried in the DOM structure
  • Perfect for catching sneaky content that passes visual inspection
