GBP Review Scraper

Extract Google Business Profile reviews from Google Search and Maps with one click. Free Chrome extension that exports to CSV instantly.

Download Extension

Download v1.0 from the Chrome Store.

How it works

  • Install the Chrome extension following the steps below
  • Navigate to any Google Business listing on Google Search or Maps
  • Click the extension icon in your toolbar
  • Watch the side panel extract reviews in real time
  • Download your reviews as a CSV file

Limitations: Requires Chrome 114+ for Side Panel API. Google structure changes may occasionally break functionality.

Complete review data

Every CSV export includes five essential columns that capture the full review context.

  • ✓ Name – Reviewer's display name
  • ✓ Rating – Star rating (e.g., "5 stars")
  • ✓ Date – When the review was posted
  • ✓ Review – Full review text
  • ✓ Response – Business owner's reply (if any)

Common questions

Most issues have simple fixes. Here's what to check if something's not working.

Extension icon is greyed out

The extension only activates on Google Search or Maps pages with a business listing. Navigate to a valid listing and try again.

'Something went wrong' error

Google may have changed their page structure. Wait for the page to fully load, or check saijogeorge.com/gbp-review-scraper for an updated version.

Scraping is very slow

Listings with 500+ reviews will take longer. The extension scrolls through all reviews to ensure complete extraction. This is normal behaviour.
