Request Access to Service Area Business (SAB) on Google

Our "Request Access to Service Area Business (SAB) on Google" tool eliminates the need for complex workarounds. Simply run the bookmarklet on any Google SERP or Map page where you see the business profile listed and you will get an option to ask for access.


Request Access to GBP

Installation Instructions:

Drag the button above to your bookmarks bar. If you don't see the bookmarks bar, enable it in your browser settings.

How To Use:

  • Google the business name and make sure the Google Business Profile listing shows up (will work in SERPs or maps pages), run the bookmarklet.
  • A popup should appear, allowing you to copy the PlaceID or go to the page where you can request access to the Google Business Profile.
  • Fill out the requested info.
  • Submit the form.

Some Limitations:

  • There could be some instances where it does not work.
